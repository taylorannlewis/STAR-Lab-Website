Behavior Analysis at MTSU

Middle Tennessee State University offers undergraduate and graduate opportunities for students interested in behavior analysis. Whether you're exploring the field for the first time or preparing for advanced training, MTSU provides coursework, research opportunities, and faculty mentorship grounded in the science of behavior. Our Faculty

Behavior Analysis Faculty

Behavior analysis at MTSU is supported by a collaborative group of faculty whose research spans basic, translational, and applied behavior analysis. Together, our laboratories provide students with diverse opportunities to engage in research while benefiting from a shared commitment to scientific rigor, meaningful mentorship, and improving socially significant outcomes.
